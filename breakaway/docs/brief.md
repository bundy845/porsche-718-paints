# Breakaway — creative brief

Started 2026-10-19. Current direction: Violet / lime paint breakaway, r003. The initial hexagon direction was rejected after in-car review.

## Agreed direction

- Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 Clubsport; reuse the exact 2048 × 2048, 8-bit RGB template identified by the shared mapping profile.
- One layered master for Rick and Justin. Rick: violet primary `#7025ED`, lime secondary `#C7FF18`. Justin: lime primary, violet secondary. Shared charcoal `#17191E`.
- Shared composition with explicit editable driver color controls; reverse color roles while retaining front-to-rear movement.
- Follow the third original user reference: solid front paint breaks away into another color through fine, irregular rearward streaks and distressed flecks. Secondary color takes over toward the rear. Use the reference's visual principle; its original example was blue to black.
- No hexagons, wedges, cubes, cell grids or floating geometric fragments. Keep the front quiet and let a broad texture transition develop through the sides and roof into the rear deck. Avoid a hard vertical cutoff.
- r003 revision: earlier onset reaching the fenders with corresponding hood extent, longer breakup into the rear bumper, and greater variation in patch size/density. The exact hood/fender join must be verified with the targeted numbered diagnostic.
- Continue the surrounding paint onto separately mapped handles, mirror shells, intake/duct surrounds and wing faces using their individual orientations. Wing should be secondary yellow/lime with sparse primary purple; black was not requested. Unmapped inner mirror parts need evidence before painting.
- Correct mapped part omissions seen on the r001 screenshots: front louver/duct surrounds, handles, windshield banner, rear center inset, rear deck lips and wing inner faces. Cover all confirmed exterior mapping entries while retaining protection and leaving unknown islands unassigned.
- Door-number areas stay visually quiet. Rear number sits over the rear paint color; remove the contrasting rectangular r001 patch.
- Pattern only for the first review. Team logos, personal graphics and sponsors are deferred until after review.
- Supplied examples and the generated comparison sheet are inspiration, not artwork to copy. The chosen column was the exploding direction.
